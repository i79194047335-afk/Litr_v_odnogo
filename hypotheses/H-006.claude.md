HYPOTHESIS: On 31 Jul → 1 Aug 2026, restricted to accounts clearing 50 fills on
every day 29 Jul – 2 Aug, daily realised-PnL rank correlation stands above its
permutation null on markets 0, 2 and 24 (A); on market 0 this is a rank effect
and not a magnitude effect, Spearman +0.581 vs Pearson +0.023 (B); and
daily-PnL ranking is rejected as a selection mechanism, three of four adjacent
pairs being noise (C).

VERDICT: SUPPORTED for A and C. SUPPORTED-AS-OBSERVATION but OVERSTATED-AS-WORDED
for B.

GHOST-NUMBERS: (none found among the figures I can trace, but see CONFLICTS
WITH RECORD — the corpus sent to the falsifier could not verify any of them)

UNSTATED ASSUMPTIONS:
  - That `min_fills=50` is the right eligibility threshold. It is inherited from
    earlier work in this track, not derived here. The anomalous pair was re-run
    at 100 and 200 (it strengthens); the other three pairs were NOT, so "three
    pairs are noise" holds at 50 only.
  - That daily realised PnL per account is the right unit at all. An account is
    not necessarily a trader (sub-accounts, farms — `API_DIGEST.md` records one
    wallet holding thousands of accounts at 33% of the sample by count and 0.3%
    by turnover).
  - That the permutation null in `pnl_persistence.compare` remains the correct
    null after the population is restricted externally. The statistic and the
    null are computed on the same restricted set, which I believe is right, but
    it is an assumption of the tool, not a proven property.
  - That realised PnL is comparable across a day where activity halves. Volume
    falls by half on all three markets across this break; a rank computed on
    thinner activity is a noisier estimate of the same quantity.

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS:
  - Heavy tails, for B. Pearson on daily PnL is dominated by a few large
    accounts; near-zero Pearson beside strong Spearman is what these two
    statistics do on such a variable. This does NOT establish "magnitudes carry
    no information" — only that no linear relationship summarises them. The
    wording in `H-005.result.md` is stronger than the evidence and needs
    softening regardless of this round's outcome.
  - Multiple testing, for A. Twelve checks at 5% expect 0.6 false alarms; three
    were observed. The concentration on one pair is suggestive but NO p-value is
    claimable: the three markets share accounts and are not independent tests.
    If the falsifier computes such a p-value, that is an error.
  - Regime, for A — a property of those two days rather than of participants.
    This is what H-005 tried to pin down and failed: its compositional mechanism
    was refuted by its own pre-registered criterion. No mechanism is offered in
    its place, so "regime" is a label, not an explanation.
  - Survivorship, for A: accounts that traded all five days are those that did
    not blow up. Partly addressed below, not fully.

ARITHMETIC: Recomputed independently on the one objection that could have
invalidated A — that the core was selected using both days of the correlated
pair. Rebuilding the core from ONLY the three days outside the pair (29, 30 Jul,
2 Aug) gives, per market: m0 core 174, common 143, ρ +0.5813 (null p95 +0.1369);
m2 core 60, common 56, ρ +0.3551 (p95 +0.2267); m24 core 58, common 53, ρ +0.4002
(p95 +0.2221). Identical to four decimals to the in-pair-selected core.
Converges: selection on activity does not induce the correlation. Core sizes and
ρ values elsewhere in the hypothesis reproduce on re-run.

KILL-SHOT: Two, cheapest first.
  1. For B, and it is decisive: recompute the magnitude relationship with a
     rank-robust alternative to Pearson — Spearman on |PnL|, or Pearson after
     winsorising at the 5th/95th percentile, or simply drop the top 3 accounts by
     |PnL| on each day and recompute both statistics. If Pearson rises toward
     Spearman once outliers are tamed, "rank not magnitude" is dead and the
     effect is ordinary correlation obscured by tails. Costs one script over
     data already on disk.
  2. For A and C together: the second pair. 4 Aug closes tonight. If
     `0803→0804` (both complete) shows ρ above null synchronously on three
     markets AND the same Spearman/Pearson gap, the observation becomes a claim
     worth pursuing; if it is noise like the other three pairs, this stays a
     single unexplained pair and should be left alone. Note 3 Aug covered only
     18.4 h and must be re-checked for completeness before use.
