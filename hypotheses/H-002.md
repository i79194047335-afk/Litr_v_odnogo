# H-002: participant tape — role split and account types

Status: open
Proposed: 2026-08-01
Proposer: Claude

## Claim

On Lighter's BTC market (market_id 1), participants do not switch between maker
and taker roles: among accounts with at least 100 role-participations in a day,
those that rest ≥95% of the time and those that cross ≥95% of the time together
account for roughly three quarters of two-sided notional, with few accounts in
between.

Separately: the `account_type` field returned by the venue's `/account` endpoint
takes four distinct values across the accounts seen in the tape, and one of them
marks an account whose L1 address is the zero address, which trades a
non-trivial share of BTC volume.

## Notes for the falsifier

Scope and field semantics only.

- One tape record is ONE fill naming both sides: `ask_account_id` and
  `bid_account_id`. `is_maker_ask` says which side rested.
- Both sides are credited with the fill's notional, so summing per-account
  notional gives TWICE the market's one-sided turnover. "Two-sided notional"
  always means that doubled quantity.
- "Role-participations" (`fills` in the report) counts an account once per role
  it played, so the total is exactly twice the record count.
- `size` and `price` arrive as decimal strings; notional is their product.
- `account_type` is read from the venue's REST `/account?by=index` response, not
  inferred from the id.
- Only whole closed UTC days were aggregated for per-day figures; the 2026-08-01
  files were excluded from those.
- The three-day cross-market figures cover 2026-07-29..07-31.
- An earlier figure of "$14.3bn sub-account volume" was withdrawn: it came from a
  jq expression where `as $n` bound only `(.price|tonumber)`, so prices were
  summed instead of notionals. The numbers above post-date that fix.
