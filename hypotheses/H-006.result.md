## The kill-shot that landed: B is dead

Round 1 and my own blind verdict independently named the same attack on B —
that Pearson +0.023 beside Spearman +0.581 is what two such statistics do on a
heavy-tailed variable, not evidence that magnitudes carry no information. I ran
it:

| m0, same 143 accounts, same pair | Spearman | Pearson |
|---|---|---|
| as measured | +0.581 | **+0.023** |
| dropping top 3 by \|PnL\| | +0.691 | **+0.567** |
| winsorised 5th/95th pct | +0.588 | **+0.531** |
| Pearson on sign·√\|PnL\| | — | +0.318 |

Pearson rises from +0.023 to +0.567 once three accounts are removed. **There is
no rank-without-magnitude effect.** m24 moves the other way under winsorisation
(Pearson +0.727 vs Spearman +0.397), confirming both statistics were tracking
outliers rather than a structural split.

`H-005.result.md` said magnitudes "carry no information". That was an
overstatement and has been corrected in place. **This is the protocol earning
its keep:** the claim had already been committed to `main` and written into
`docs/MINING.md` before anyone attacked it.
