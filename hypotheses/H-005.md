# H-005: why daily-PnL ranking became predictable on 31 Jul → 1 Aug

Status: open
Proposed: 2026-08-03
Proposer: Claude

## Background, and what is already settled

The pass criterion recorded in `MINING.md` §5 has been run on complete days.
Daily-PnL ranking is **rejected** as a selection mechanism: of four pairs, three
sit in the null band and only `0731→0801` is above noise, synchronously on all
three markets.

| pair | m0 | m2 | m24 | above noise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29→30 Jul | +0.0040 | −0.2142 | −0.1056 | 0 of 3 |
| 30→31 Jul | −0.0575 | −0.3083 | −0.0970 | 0 of 3 |
| **31 Jul→1 Aug** | **+0.4229** | **+0.3176** | **+0.3877** | **3 of 3** |
| 1→2 Aug | −0.0326 | −0.1551 | +0.2231 | 1 of 3 |

(Measured with `min_fills=50`, `pnl_persistence.py`. The earlier figures in
`MINING.md` for `0801→0802` — m0 +0.116, m24 +0.578 — were taken while 1 Aug
was still being collected and are superseded: that file now covers a full 24.0 h.)

Raising `min_fills` 50 → 200 **strengthens** the anomalous pair (m0 +0.42 →
+0.57, m24 +0.39 → +0.55) while leaving the others in noise, so it is not an
artefact of who passes the threshold.

This hypothesis is about the one unexplained thing left in the data. It is
**not** an attempt to revive ranking as a selection mechanism.

## Claim

**A.** The synchronous rank correlation on `0731→0801` is a property of those
two days, not of the participants. Specifically: it is carried by a *population*
change rather than by the same traders repeating their results.

**B.** The mechanism is compositional. 31 July is the activity peak of the
sample (m24: 44 527 fills, $37.3M, 857 accounts) and 1 August drops sharply on
all three markets (m24: 34 104 fills, $18.0M, 630 accounts). When a burst of
small transient accounts leaves, the surviving pool is dominated by a stable
core whose relative ordering is mechanically similar across the two days —
producing rank correlation without any per-trader persistence.

## Discriminating criterion, written BEFORE the measurement

Two mechanisms predict opposite things, so the measurement distinguishes rather
than merely confirms (`WORKFLOW.md` §4a).

- **If compositional (B):** restricting both days to accounts that traded on
  *all five* days 29 Jul – 2 Aug should **remove or sharply reduce** the
  correlation on `0731→0801`, because the transient population is what carried
  it. Threshold: ρ falls into the null band (below that pair's own p95) on at
  least 2 of 3 markets.
- **If genuine per-trader persistence:** the same restriction should **preserve
  or increase** ρ, since a stable core is exactly the population in which
  persistence ought to show. Threshold: ρ stays above p95 on at least 2 of 3
  markets.

Either outcome is informative. If the result is mixed (exactly one market moves
each way), the claim is **not** resolved and no verdict is written — the honest
reading is that 5 days is too short, which is itself worth recording.

**Secondary check, same run:** whether the accounts driving the correlation are
net-PnL-positive on both days or merely *ranked* consistently. Rank correlation
can be produced by a stable ordering of losers.

## What would falsify each part

- **A** is falsified if the correlation survives every population restriction
  and reappears on a later pair with comparable magnitude.
- **B** is falsified if restricting to the five-day core preserves ρ (that is
  the "genuine persistence" branch above).
- Both are falsified if the effect turns out to be an artefact of the tool —
  e.g. if `pnl_persistence` handles a day with missing hours differently from a
  complete one. 2 Aug is complete; 3 Aug is **not** (18.4 h, collector running)
  and is excluded from every verdict here.

## Known limits, stated up front

- Five complete days is a very short sample. A single pair standing out of four
  is not remarkable on its own; what makes it worth one measurement is the
  synchrony across three independent markets plus the strengthening with
  threshold.
- No L2 book data exists for these days (book collection began 3 Aug), so
  nothing here can be cross-checked against microstructure.
- This measurement cannot establish *what* happened in the market on those
  days. It can only distinguish "population changed" from "traders repeated".
