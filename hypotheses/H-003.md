# H-003: PnL reconstruction from the tape, and whether it ranks participants

Status: open
Proposed: 2026-08-02
Proposer: Claude

## Claim

**A.** Realised PnL per account can be reconstructed from the public
`trade/{market}` WS feed alone. The position arithmetic it rests on predicts
the venue's own next reported `*_position_size_before` and
`*_entry_quote_before` for 99.956%–99.995% of checks across 4 markets on 2 days.

**B.** That reconstruction does **not** by itself identify persistently
profitable participants on a per-day basis. Overlap of the top-20 by realised
PnL between two consecutive days is 4/20 on market 24 and 5/20 on market 2,
against a random-draw baseline whose 95th percentile is 5 on both. On market 0
the overlap is 8/20 against a baseline p95 of 3.

## Notes for the falsifier

Scope and mechanics, no argument:

- The WS feed carries, per fill, both sides' `*_position_size_before` and
  `*_entry_quote_before` as reported by the venue. It carries no PnL field:
  `ask_account_pnl` / `bid_account_pnl` are documented at
  https://apidocs.lighter.xyz/reference/trades.md but never arrive on this
  channel. PnL is therefore computed, and there is no venue figure to compare
  a total against.
- Fees are excluded from all PnL figures. The unit of `maker_fee` / `taker_fee`
  is not specified in the schema and has not been established by measurement.
  All PnL numbers above are gross.
- "Realised" means closing PnL only. Open positions are not marked to market;
  the tape carries no reference price for doing so.
- Records within one WS frame are stored newest-first. All figures above are
  computed after sorting by `(transaction_time, trade_id)`.
- `min_fills=20` throughout. On market 24, 20260731 that is 236 of 857
  accounts, which carry 95.9% of notional.
- The random-draw baseline samples two independent sets of 20 from the
  accounts present on both days, and counts the intersection.
- Claim A and Claim B are separate. A is about arithmetic against the venue;
  B is about whether the resulting ranking repeats across days.
