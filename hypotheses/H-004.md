# H-004: public pools as an external check on reconstructed PnL

Status: open
Proposed: 2026-08-02
Proposer: Claude

## Claim

**A.** `account_type` 2 identifies a public pool operator and `account_type` 3
identifies a pool run by the venue itself. All 319 pools enumerable from
`publicPoolsMetadata` carry type 2 or 3, and the two type-3 pools are
`Lighter Liquidity Provider (LLP)` and `Experimental Liquidity Provider (XLP)`,
both with L1 address `0x0000…0000`.

**B.** The PnL reconstructed by `src/analysis/participant_pnl.py` agrees in
sign with the venue's published annual return for those pools. Of 11 pools that
traded on markets 0/1/2/24 during 2026-07-28..08-02, 9 agree in sign
(P = 0.033 against a coin flip); Spearman between published APR and our 5-day
realised PnL is +0.52. Restricted to the 9 pools with at least 20 fills — the
same threshold used elsewhere in this track — agreement is 9 of 9 (P = 0.002)
and Spearman is +0.72.

## Notes for the falsifier

Scope and mechanics, no argument:

- Public Pools is Lighter's copy-trading product: an operator trades, others
  deposit, the operator takes `operator_fee` of the profit. The showcase
  publishes `name`, `annual_percentage_yield`, `sharpe_ratio`,
  `total_asset_value`, `created_at` and the operator's `account_index`.
- The published APR and our figure are **not commensurable**: APR is annual,
  over pool lifetimes of 150–562 days, net of operator fee, with deposits and
  withdrawals moving the base. Ours is 5 days, gross, realised-only, restricted
  to 4 of roughly 90 markets, and counts only fills where the pool is one side.
  Claim B therefore asserts agreement of **sign and rank order only**.
- The showcase's own numbers are unverified. One pool reports `sharpe_ratio`
  12.31 alongside APR 30.02%.
- `fills>=20` is the threshold already used by `participant_pnl` and
  `pnl_persistence` throughout the track; it was not chosen after seeing this
  result. Both figures (11-pool and 9-pool) are reported.
- Before this, reconstructed PnL had no external referent at all: the WS feed
  carries no PnL field (`ask_account_pnl` / `bid_account_pnl` are documented
  but never arrive — 0 of 300,000 records), and REST PnL for a foreign account
  returns 400.
- Claim A and Claim B are separate. A is about what the account types mean;
  B is about whether our arithmetic agrees with an outside number.
