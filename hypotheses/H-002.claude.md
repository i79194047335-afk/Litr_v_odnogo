# H-002 — Claude's verdict (written blind, before unseal)

VERDICT: PARTLY REFUTED

The account-type half survives. The role-split half is true on BTC as stated
but was generalised further than the data supports, and one of its words is
wrong.

## What holds

**Account types.** Four distinct values (0, 1, 2, 3) across 9,196 accounts, and
the correspondence with the id ranges is exact in both directions: every type-0
id is below 1e9 (4723/4723), every type-1/2/3 id sits just under 2^48
(4473/4473). Read from the venue's own `/account` response, not inferred.

**The zero-address account.** Exactly one account carries
`l1_address 0x0000…0000`, id 281474976710654, type 3. Re-measured per day rather
than once: 0.94% (Jul 29), 0.86% (Jul 30), 1.99% (Jul 31) of two-sided BTC
notional, and 100% maker on all three. "Non-trivial share" holds; the single
figure of 2.0% quoted in the claim is the largest of the three days and should
not be cited alone.

**Turnover and fills** for BTC 2026-07-31 were checked twice by separate code
paths and agree to the dollar, and the identity that maker notional equals taker
notional holds, as it must when both sides of one fill carry one value.

## What does not hold

**"Few accounts in between" is false as written.** On BTC Jul 31 there are 392
mid-range accounts (5% < maker share < 95%) against 98 pure makers — the middle
group is the *larger* of the two by count. It is smaller only by volume (18.3%
against 41.4%). The claim conflated "few" with "small", and the word chosen was
the one the data does not support.

**"Roughly three quarters" is a one-day figure presented as a property.** Across
the three closed days on BTC the pure-role share is 68.5% / 82.7% / 76.8% — a
14-point spread, so 76.8% is not a stable constant but the middle of a moving
band.

**The generalisation to "participants" fails on one of the four markets.** The
claim names BTC only, and on BTC/ETH/SOL the pure share runs 74–86%. But on
HYPE it is 45.7%, with 40.4% mid-range — the opposite structure. Anything
written as a property of "Lighter participants" rather than of BTC would be
refuted by HYPE. The claim is narrow enough to survive this literally, and the
surrounding session commentary was not.

## Ghosts found

None. Every number in the table traces to a file or a runnable script, and the
one figure that had no honest source ($14.3bn) had already been withdrawn and is
recorded as withdrawn in the hypothesis itself.

## What would change the verdict

- Per-day and per-market spreads reported beside every role-split figure, and
  "few" replaced with "smaller by volume" — then the claim reads as measured.
- The type-3 account's role identified from the venue (insurance fund?
  liquidator?). Its behaviour is consistent with a system account, but that is
  inference from 100%-maker and a zero address, not documentation. Type 2 —
  ten accounts, near-zero volume — remains undocumented and unexplained.

## Weakest point of my own position

Three days is a short base, and all three are adjacent. The stability I claim
for the account-type mapping is strong (an exact partition on 9,196 accounts),
but the role-split numbers rest on one market-week and could move with a
volatility regime the sample does not contain. Sample size is the honest
limit here, not the arithmetic.
