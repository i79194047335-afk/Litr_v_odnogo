## The criterion was written first, and it went against me

`H-005.md` fixed this before any measurement:

> **If compositional (B):** restricting both days to accounts that traded on
> *all five* days should remove or sharply reduce the correlation. Threshold:
> ρ falls into the null band on at least 2 of 3 markets.
> **If genuine per-trader persistence:** the restriction should preserve or
> increase ρ. Threshold: ρ stays above p95 on at least 2 of 3 markets.

Measured, `min_fills=50`, core = accounts clearing the threshold on **every**
day 29 Jul – 2 Aug:

| market | core | ρ full population | ρ core only | null p95 (core) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 143 | +0.4229 | **+0.5813** | +0.1369 | above |
| 2 | 56 | +0.3176 | **+0.3551** | +0.2267 | above |
| 24 | 53 | +0.3877 | **+0.4002** | +0.2221 | above |

**3 of 3 above the null band. B is refuted.** The correlation is not carried by
a departing transient population; it survives, and on m0 it strengthens
(+0.42 → +0.58). My compositional explanation was wrong.

## The obvious confound was checked and ruled out

If core-restriction lifted correlations generally, the result would be an
artefact of the restriction rather than a property of those days. It does not:

| pair | m0 core | m2 core | m24 core |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29→30 Jul | −0.027 | −0.207 | −0.155 |
| 30→31 Jul | −0.306 | −0.207 | −0.266 |
| **31 Jul→1 Aug** | **+0.581** | **+0.355** | **+0.400** |
| 1→2 Aug | −0.180 | −0.195 | −0.059 |

Restriction leaves the other three pairs in noise and in several cases pushes
them *more negative*. Only the one pair survives, on all three markets.

**This tightens the earlier rejection.** m24's marginal `0801→0802` (+0.223,
p95 +0.181) collapses to −0.059 on the core, so that pair is now unambiguously
noise. Daily-PnL ranking is rejected on **every** pair except `0731→0801`.

## ~~The actual finding: rank persists, magnitude does not~~ — WITHDRAWN

The heading above is kept struck through rather than deleted: it is what this
file claimed until 2026-08-04, it reached `docs/MINING.md`, and deleting it
would hide that the correction happened. The numbers below are real; the
reading of them was not.

The gap that looked like the finding:

| market | n | Spearman | Pearson | top-quintile retained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 143 | **+0.581** | **+0.023** | 19/28 |
| 2 | 56 | +0.355 | +0.410 | 6/11 |
| 24 | 53 | +0.400 | +0.412 | 6/10 |

**CORRECTED 2026-08-04 — this reading was wrong.** The sentence originally here
said magnitudes "carry no information (+0.023)". That over-read a heavy-tail
artefact. Both H-006's falsifier round and my own pre-unseal verdict named this
attack, and the measurement settles it against me:

| m0, same 143 accounts, same pair | Spearman | Pearson |
|---|---|---|
| as measured | +0.581 | **+0.023** |
| dropping top 3 by \|PnL\| | +0.691 | **+0.567** |
| winsorised at 5th/95th pct | +0.588 | **+0.531** |

Pearson near zero was produced by a handful of extreme accounts, not by absent
magnitude information. Once the tails are tamed, Pearson rises to the same order
as Spearman. **There is no rank-without-magnitude effect** — the m24 column moves
the other way too (winsorised Pearson +0.727 against Spearman +0.397), which is
further evidence that these statistics were tracking outliers rather than a
structural split.

What survives: the *ordering* correlation on this pair is real and above its null
(that part was attacked directly and held, see H-006). Its interpretation as
"rank but not size" does not survive. See `hypotheses/H-006.result.md`.

## What A claims, and how far it is supported

A said the effect is a property of those two days rather than of the
participants. Half of that survives:

- **Supported:** the effect is confined to one pair out of four. Neighbouring
  pairs, on the same accounts, are in noise or negative.
- **Not supported:** the mechanism. B was the only concrete mechanism proposed
  and it is refuted. What made those two days different is **unknown**.

Volume context, measured but explaining nothing on its own: 31 July is the
sample's activity peak and 1 August drops on all three markets
(m24 $37.3M → $18.0M, 857 → 630 accounts; m0 $287M → $102M). Both days sit
either side of that break. This is a description, not a mechanism — a
correlation between "activity halved" and "ranking held" cannot be established
from one occurrence.

## Why no protocol round

`CLAUDE.md` §4 requires the consensus protocol when a claim carries a number
**and** the next decision leans on it. This result carries numbers but **no
decision leans on it**: it neither revives ranking as a selection mechanism nor
proposes a strategy. It closes a loose end and records an unexplained residue.

Should anyone later propose trading on rank persistence, that proposal needs the
protocol — and it starts from a sample of one day-pair, which is why it should
not be proposed yet.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **One pair out of four.** Twelve checks at a 5% false-positive rate expect
  0.6 false alarms; three were observed, all on one pair. Whether that
  concentration is itself unlikely **cannot be computed** here: the three
  markets share accounts, so they are not independent tests. The synchrony is
  suggestive, not established.
- **Five complete days.** 3 Aug was excluded from every verdict: 18.4 h of 24,
  collector still running.
- **No microstructure.** Book collection began 3 Aug, so nothing about those
  days can be cross-checked against the order book — the one tool that might
  identify what changed.
- **Not tradeable as it stands.** One pair is not a sample. (The original
  wording here — "rank without magnitude does not size a position" — rested on
  the withdrawn reading above; the conclusion stands, its stated reason does not.)

## What would settle it

The measurement that decides this does not exist yet: another day-pair with the
same signature. Concretely — if a future pair shows ρ above the null band
synchronously on all three markets **and** the same Spearman-without-Pearson
gap, then "rank persists in some regimes" becomes a real claim worth the
protocol. Until then this is one observation, recorded so it is not
rediscovered and not overclaimed.
