## What was actually wrong

1. **"Few accounts in between" — false.** 392 mid-range accounts against 98
   pure makers on BTC 07-31: the middle group is *larger* by count, smaller
   only by volume (18.3% vs 41.4%). Both reviewers caught this independently.
2. **"Roughly three quarters" was a single day.** Across the three closed BTC
   days the pure-role share is 68.5% / 82.7% / 76.8% — a 14-point band, not a
   constant.
3. **HYPE does not behave like BTC.** Pure share 45.7% against BTC's 76.8%,
   mid-range 40.4%. Any statement about "Lighter participants" rather than
   about BTC is refuted by HYPE — even though role persistence itself still
   holds there.
4. **Round 2's procedural hit was correct.** Four figures in the blind verdict
   (per-day type-3 share, the three-day spread, HYPE, the 392 count) were
   computed in throwaway shell and left no artifact. Measured but untraceable
   is the ghost pattern in section 2 of CLAUDE.md. Fixed: they are now produced
   by `role_persistence.py` with output committed under `H-002.artifacts/`.

## What holds

- **account_type has four values**, and the partition against id range is exact
  in both directions on all 9,196 accounts: type 0 ↔ id < 1e9 (4723/4723),
  types 1/2/3 ↔ ids just under 2^48 (4473/4473).
- **One account carries the zero L1 address** (`281474976710654`, type 3),
  is 100% maker every day, and takes 0.94% / 0.86% / 1.99% of two-sided BTC
  notional on 07-29 / 07-30 / 07-31. Behaviour consistent with a venue system
  account; the venue does not document it, so that reading stays an inference.
- **Type 2 — ten accounts, near-zero volume — remains unexplained.** Not in the
  docs the archivarius has mirrored. Open question, not a conclusion.
- Turnover and fill counts for BTC 07-31 agree to the dollar across two
  independent code paths, and maker notional equals taker notional as it must.

## The honest limit

Three adjacent days, one venue, one volatility regime. The account-type
partition is strong evidence (an exact split over 9,196 accounts). Role
persistence is now measured on three day-pairs across two markets rather than
asserted — but a week is not a regime, and nothing here says what these accounts
do when the market moves hard.
